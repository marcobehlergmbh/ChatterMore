# ChatterMore Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

ChatterMore is an unofficial Android client compatible with Mattermost. It is
provided by Marco Behler GmbH ("we", "us", or "our").

This policy explains what information ChatterMore handles, where that
information goes, and what choices you have.

## Summary

- ChatterMore does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or
  crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not operate a ChatterMore backend and do not receive your Mattermost
  credentials, messages, or attachments.
- The app connects directly to the Mattermost server you choose.
- Some data is stored locally on your Android device so the app can reconnect,
  display recent content, and provide notifications.
- Links and media previews may contact the websites that host those resources.

## Information handled by the app

Depending on how you use ChatterMore, the app may handle:

- The Mattermost server URL you enter.
- Your Mattermost username, password, user ID, display name, and session token.
- Team, channel, direct-message, membership, and presence information.
- Messages and message metadata that you read, send, or edit.
- Photos, videos, audio, and other files that you view, upload, share, or
  download.
- Notification preferences and limited message information needed for
  background message checks.

ChatterMore does not create or manage your Mattermost account. Your account and
server-side data are controlled by the operator of the Mattermost server you
choose.

## How information is used

ChatterMore uses this information only to provide app functionality, including:

- Authenticating with your selected Mattermost server.
- Loading conversations, messages, profiles, presence information, and
  attachments.
- Sending and editing messages and uploading files at your request.
- Remembering your connection and recent conversation state.
- Checking for new messages and displaying notifications when you enable that
  feature.
- Displaying link and media previews.

## Network transfers

ChatterMore requires an HTTPS Mattermost server and rejects cleartext HTTP
connections. Your credentials and app content are transferred directly between
your device and the server you configured.

The Mattermost server operator may process and retain this information
according to its own privacy policy, security settings, and retention rules.
Contact that operator for questions about server-side information.

## External links and previews

Messages can contain links or media hosted outside your Mattermost server.
ChatterMore may contact external hosts to display or play that content:

- YouTube thumbnail servers may be contacted when a message contains a YouTube
  link.
- A website hosting an Open Graph preview image may be contacted to load that
  image.
- Suno pages and media servers may be contacted when you use Suno playback.
- Your browser or another installed app is used when you choose to open an
  external link.

Those services can receive ordinary network information such as your IP
address, request time, and user-agent information. Their own privacy policies
govern their handling of that information.

## Information stored on your device

ChatterMore stores limited information locally to provide its features:

- Your saved password and background session token are encrypted using a key
  held by Android Keystore.
- A recent conversation snapshot is encrypted on the device.
- Server, account, channel, and conversation metadata may be stored in
  app-private storage.
- Image and media previews may be stored in a bounded app-private cache.
- Notification settings and background-check state are stored locally.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for ChatterMore.

Clearing the saved connection removes saved credentials and the background
session. Some non-secret connection metadata and cached files can remain until
they are replaced, evicted, or removed by clearing ChatterMore's storage in
Android settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-private local data.

## Files and notifications

ChatterMore accesses photos, videos, and documents only when you select or
share them with the app. Camera capture is delegated to a compatible camera
app. ChatterMore does not request broad access to all files on your device.

When you confirm a download, the selected attachment is saved through Android's
download service and may remain in your public Downloads collection after
ChatterMore is uninstalled.

If you enable background message checks, Android notifications may contain
sender and message-preview information according to your ChatterMore and
Android notification settings. Notification contents may be visible on a
locked device depending on your Android settings.

## Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. ChatterMore has no advertising SDKs and
does not send your activity to us for analytics or profiling.

Information is sent to your selected Mattermost server, and to external content
hosts when required to perform an action or load content as described above.

## Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain a ChatterMore backend containing your app data.

You can remove locally stored ChatterMore data by clearing the app's storage in
Android settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing the connection inside the
app removes saved credentials but does not delete your Mattermost account,
messages, files, or other server-side data.

To access, correct, export, or delete information stored on a Mattermost
server, contact that server's operator or administrator.

## Security

ChatterMore uses HTTPS, Android Keystore-backed encryption for saved secrets,
encrypted storage for its recent conversation snapshot, app-private storage,
and disabled Android backup to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are
responsible for choosing a trusted Mattermost server and protecting access to
your device and account.

## Children's privacy

ChatterMore is not directed to children. The operator of each Mattermost server
is responsible for deciding who may use that server and for obtaining any
consent required for its users.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ChatterMore's features or data handling change.
The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision.
