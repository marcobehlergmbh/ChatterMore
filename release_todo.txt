1. Choose how to distribute the app
-----------------------------------

Choose one of these routes before doing the remaining work:

A. Public Google Play app

Use this if anyone with a compatible Mattermost server should be able to find
and install the app. All of the technical, legal, store-listing, privacy, UGC
moderation, testing, and review work below applies.

B. Managed Google Play private app

Use this if the app is only for Marco Behler GmbH, its staff, or specified
customer organizations using managed Android devices. It stays out of the
public store and is considerably simpler to distribute.

Private-app guide:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9874937?hl=en-GB


2. Developer account recommendation
-----------------------------------

Recommendation: publish it through the Marco Behler GmbH organization account.

Reasons:

- The GmbH is the appropriate long-term owner if this is a professional
  product, company tool, customer offering, or anything the company will
  maintain and support.
- The public developer identity, support contact, contracts, privacy
  responsibility, trademark position, and possible future revenue all remain
  with the company rather than with Marco personally.
- Team members can be given Play Console access without sharing a personal
  Google account.
- Ownership will be cleaner if the app later becomes commercial, is handed to
  employees, or is transferred as part of the business.
- It avoids a potentially awkward app transfer from a personal account later.

Use a personal account only if this will genuinely remain a personal,
non-commercial hobby project that is legally and operationally separate from
Marco Behler GmbH.

Organization-account preparation:

- Confirm that the GmbH already has the correct D-U-N-S number.
- Ensure the company name and address in the Google Payments profile exactly
  match the Dun & Bradstreet record.
- Use a company-controlled Google account as the account owner.
- Use durable company email addresses and telephone numbers.
- Enable two-step verification.
- Add individual team members through Play Console permissions rather than
  sharing the owner login.

Google notes that obtaining a new D-U-N-S number can take up to 30 days:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312

7. Create the privacy and legal material
----------------------------------------

Every Play app needs a public privacy-policy URL and access to the policy
inside the app, even if the publisher determines that it does not collect
user data.

Document the exact roles carefully. The app connects directly to a Mattermost
server selected by the user, but the publisher, the Mattermost server operator,
and external content providers may have different privacy responsibilities.
Do not guess when completing the Data Safety form.

The privacy policy should accurately cover:

- Mattermost server address, team, channel, username, and password.
- Messages, usernames, avatars, files, presence, and related channel data.
- The encrypted local password and locally stored connection configuration.
- Cached avatars, message images, link-preview images, and downloaded files.
- Direct network communication with the selected Mattermost server.
- Automatic YouTube thumbnail requests.
- Suno page and audio requests initiated by playback.
- External link-preview image requests, including arbitrary HTTPS hosts
  supplied through post metadata.
- The fact that external hosts can receive IP address and ordinary network
  request metadata.
- Purposes, legal basis, recipients, retention, security measures, and
  international transfers where applicable.
- User rights and how to contact the data controller.
- How local data can be erased.
- Which data is controlled by the user's Mattermost administrator rather than
  by the app publisher.

App changes:

- Add a clearly visible Privacy link inside the app.
- Add Legal/Terms and Open Source Notices screens or links.
- Add "Clear all local data," including credentials and the disk image cache.
- Consider loading external preview images only after user consent/tap, or
  proxying them through an appropriately disclosed service.

The app does not currently create Mattermost accounts. Therefore Google's
in-app and web account-deletion mechanism should not apply unless account
creation is added later. The Data Safety deletion questions must still be
answered accurately.

References:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111
https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/principles-gdpr/what-information-must-be-given-individuals-whose-data-collected_en

For a commercial service offered from Germany, obtain legal advice about the
provider-information requirements under section 5 DDG:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html

9. Test the actual release
--------------------------

Test the signed, minified release delivered through Google Play, not only a
debug APK.

Required test coverage:

- Android 10 through Android 16.
- Small and large phones.
- At least one tablet or large-screen emulator/device.
- Fresh install, upgrade, logout/change connection, and reinstall.
- Correct and incorrect server information.
- Expired or changed passwords.
- Slow, interrupted, and unavailable networks.
- WebSocket disconnect and reconnect.
- Message send, edit, retry, and deletion.
- Single and multiple file sharing.
- Gallery and camera attachments.
- Large file rejection and interrupted uploads.
- Image, video, file download, YouTube preview, external preview, and Suno.
- Rotation, process death, low-memory recreation, and background/foreground.
- Accessibility using TalkBack and large text/display sizes.
- A server with a valid public TLS certificate.
- Representative supported Mattermost server versions.

Run:

./gradlew testDebugUnitTest lintRelease bundleRelease --console=plain

Then upload the signed AAB to Play internal testing and test exactly what Play
delivers. Resolve relevant issues in the Play pre-launch report and Android
Vitals.

11. Prepare the Play Store listing
----------------------------------

Required product material:

- App name, maximum 30 characters.
- Short description, maximum 80 characters.
- Full description, maximum 4,000 characters.
- 512 x 512 Play Store icon.
- 1024 x 500 feature graphic.
- At least two representative phone screenshots.
- Support email address.
- Website and privacy-policy URL.
- App category and tags.
- Optional German translation and localized screenshots.

The listing should clearly state:

- This is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with Mattermost, Inc.
- An existing compatible Mattermost server and account are required.
- Only HTTPS servers with system-trusted certificates are supported.
- The app connects to one configured team/channel at a time.
- Password-based Mattermost login is currently required.
- Threads, reactions, multiple channels, offline history, background
  notifications, push notifications, and SSO are not supported.
- The minimum Android version is Android 10.
